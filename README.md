# Signal AI Search

**An open-source, AI-powered content discovery platform redefining search for the premium design, AV, and creative industries.**

---

## Vision

Signal is not another SEO gimmick. It’s a clean slate—built to elevate authentic, intelligent content from real creators, experts, and brands. We combine the clarity of **AEO (Answer Engine Optimization)** with the depth of **GEO (Generative Engine Optimization)** to create a discovery layer built for humans *and* AI.

We're building a smarter, curated alternative to algorithm-choked search engines. This is **search by signal, not noise**.

---

## What It Does

- Uses AI to surface trusted, high-integrity content—not keyword spam
- Connects users with real creators, design professionals, and trade partners
- Builds an ecosystem of curated insight, not ads or fluff
- Powers natural-language queries with vector search + GPT summaries

---

## Why Open Source?

The current system rewards manipulation. We’re building Signal as a **global, transparent, community-powered alternative**—open to developers, integrators, designers, technologists, and truth-seekers around the world.
